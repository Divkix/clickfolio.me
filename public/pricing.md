# Pricing — clickfolio.me

clickfolio.me is a free resume-to-website builder. There is one plan and it costs nothing.

## Free

- Price: $0 (free forever)
- Billing: none, no credit card required, no trial period
- Who it's for: job seekers, students, and professionals who want a portfolio website from a resume they already have
- Includes:
  - AI parsing of an uploaded PDF resume (up to 5 MB) into an editable portfolio site
  - All 12 templates, with no premium locks, no referrals, and no unlocking
  - A shareable public page at `clickfolio.me/@handle`
  - Custom @handle selection (up to 3 handle changes per 24 hours)
  - Full editing suite with auto-save
  - Privacy controls: hide phone number, hide address, hide from search engines, remove from the /explore directory
  - Account and data deletion at any time from Settings
  - 5 resume uploads per 24 hours
- Not included: custom domain support (portfolios live on the `clickfolio.me/@handle` URL) and anything that costs money — there is nothing to buy.

## Notes

- No premium tier, no ads, no upsells, and no add-ons to buy.
- Uploading a resume and watching it parse does not require an account; an account is needed to claim a `@handle`, publish, and edit later.
- clickfolio.me is open source: https://github.com/Divkix/clickfolio.me

Start: https://clickfolio.me
